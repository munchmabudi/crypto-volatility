Distance (%) = (liq_price - current_price) / current_price × 100. + means liquidation is above current price (cascade up), - means below (cascade down).
Adj. Distance (σ) = distance in terms of daily volatility (HAR-RV). A 1% move in a 100 vol token = ~0.5σ (likely). Same 1% in a 20 vol token = ~0.3σ (less likely). Lower = more likely to get hit.
Est. Move (%) = rough price impact if that cascade triggers.
Move/Vol (σ) = est. move as multiple of daily vol. This is the filter you wanted — it shows impactful liquidations relative to typical noise. A small liquidation in a high-vol token will show low Move/Vol (not worth caring about). A large liquidation in a low-vol token will show high Move/Vol (worth watching).
Magnitude (x vol) = cumulative liquidation notional / daily volume. 1x = a full day's volume. 5x = 5 days of volume. 50x+ = massive cascade risk.
CC = Close-to-Close realized vol (7d, annualized). PK = Parkinson vol (uses high/low). HAR = HAR-RV forecast.

Distance (%) = (liq_price - current_price) / current_price × 100. + means liquidation is above current price (cascade up), - means below (cascade down).
Adj. Distance (σ) = distance in terms of daily volatility (HAR-RV). A 1% move in a 100 vol token = 0.6σ (likely). A 1% move in a 20 vol token = 0.3σ (less likely).
CC = Close-to-Close realized vol (7d, annualized). PK = Parkinson vol (uses high/low). HAR = HAR-RV forecast.
Magnitude = cumulative liquidation notional / daily volume. Est. Move = rough price impact estimate.

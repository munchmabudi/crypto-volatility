Distance (%) = (liq_price - current_price) / current_price × 100. + means liquidation is above current price (cascade up), - means below (cascade down).
Adj. Distance (σ) = distance in terms of daily volatility (HAR-RV). Lower = more likely.
Est. Move (%) = rough price impact if cascade triggers (η·σ·√(Q/V), square-root law).
Move/Vol (σ) = est. move as multiple of daily vol.
Magnitude (x vol) = cumulative liquidation notional / daily volume.
CC = Close-to-Close realized vol. PK = Parkinson vol. HAR = HAR-RV forecast.
